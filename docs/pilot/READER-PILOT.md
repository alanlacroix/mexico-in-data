# Ten-weekday reader pilot

Recruit five to eight executives with operating responsibility in Mexico. Record each reader's preferred language, sector and role. Ask permission for lightweight reading feedback; do not collect sensitive business decisions or confidential company information.

Before starting, record the reader's current news sources, approximate time spent, and the business questions they most need help following. Agree the publication time, named editor, and daily time/cost budget before measuring operational success.

Send the same focused edition each weekday through the channel readers already use, once explicitly authorized. No individual chasing to manufacture repeat visits. This document is a protocol, not authorization to contact anyone.

On three selected days ask one short question: **Did this brief help you understand or prepare for anything today? Please name one example, or one thing it missed.** Allow “no” without prompting.

At the end of week two, ask:

1. On how many days this week did you choose to read it?
2. Roughly how long did it take?
3. Which connection, if any, helped you understand something you would otherwise have missed?
4. Was anything irrelevant, repetitive, overstated or hard to trust?
5. Would you choose to keep receiving it?

For one sample edition, show a headline-and-link digest using the same underlying reporting. Alternate which version readers see first. Ask which they would prefer for their morning reading and why. Treat this as qualitative feedback, not an experiment proving causal improvement.

## Decision record

| Criterion | Proposed threshold | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Added understanding | Majority name a specific useful connection and prefer the synthesis | Not measured |
| Repeat use | At least 60% read on three or more weekdays in week two without chasing | Not measured |
| Brevity and continued interest | Median reading time at most five minutes; two-thirds want to continue | Not measured |
| Trust and delivery | No material unsupported claim, false freshness or bilingual discrepancy; ten on-time editions or explicit no-material-change notes | Not measured |
| Sustainable operation | Final five editions fit the pre-agreed time/cost budget without skipped checks | Budget not yet set |

Report participant counts and examples alongside percentages. Keep individual responses private. A small selected cohort can guide a continue/revise decision but cannot establish long-term retention or willingness to pay.

If added understanding fails, improve selection and connections. If trust fails, fix the evidence and publication process. Do not add features to compensate for either failure.
