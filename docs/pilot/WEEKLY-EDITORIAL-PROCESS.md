# Ranked weekly briefing

## Product and audience

A five-minute account of material changes for country managers and senior leaders responsible for businesses in Mexico. Developments are the unit of selection; syndicated articles do not count as separate confirmations. The first candidate is explicitly a week-so-far selection, September 21–24, not a finished Friday edition or a claim of exhaustive coverage.

## Publication procedure

1. Collect accessible reporting across trade, money, regulation, demand and operating conditions. Record inaccessible sources; do not substitute snippets for article evidence.
2. Group coverage by event. Compare each event with the previously recorded state; distinguish proposals, announcements, decisions and implementation. Do not claim a historical change without evidence for both states.
3. Select at most five developments for consequence, relevance to the audience, timing and novelty. Source quality is a gate, not a score. Record consequential exclusions. A ranking is editorial judgment, never a calculated probability or popularity metric.
4. Write a short overview without forcing a common economic narrative. Explain the ordering. Each development has a concise change, context, before/now comparison, a useful margin and source links. Do not force investment or operating advice.
5. Include at most three confirmed future dates with source links. Tentative meetings remain in story copy. Keep the total below five minutes. Fewer stories and dates are acceptable.
6. Add `weeklyBrief` to the edition candidate; run weekly validation and the complete release checks. The artifact hash includes the weekly material. Review and approve the exact artifact using the existing publication gate. Dated archives preserve the same weekly copy.

The optional weekly presentation works alongside the existing candidate schema. The collector and scheduled writer still produce daily candidates; they do not automatically manufacture this editorial selection. Do not change collection frequency or announce a weekly delivery schedule before an editor owns this procedure. The next implementation milestone is a reviewed full-week candidate, not additional UI.

## Reader test, before expanding

Use the same week's evidence for comparable shortlist, synthesis and sector-specific samples. Ask current executives what they already read and which item they would keep, remove, forward or investigate. No reader outreach is authorized by this document. Pilot the preferred format for four weeks and record repeat use, omissions, concrete value and editorial time. Open rates and design compliments do not establish usefulness.
