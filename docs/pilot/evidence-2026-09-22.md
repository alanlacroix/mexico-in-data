# MexicoBrief — Evidence notes — September 22, 2026

Internal candidate record. No publication authorization.

## Included: August activity nowcast

- **Primary source:** INEGI, *Indicador Oportuno de la Actividad Económica*, bulletin 597/26, dated September 22, 2026. [Accessible PDF](https://www.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/saladeprensa/boletines/2026/ioae/ioae2026_09.pdf)
- **Corroborating original report:** Clara Zepeda, La Jornada, published September 22 at 08:37. It reports the same August and July estimates and notes that official July IGAE is due September 24. [Article](https://www.jornada.com.mx/noticia/2026/09/22/economia/economia-mexicana-mantuvo-un-ritmo-de-expansion-durante-julio-y-agosto-inegi)
- **Verified facts:** August IGAE nowcast +2.5% year over year (95% interval 1.3%–3.7%) and +0.1% month over month (-1.0%–1.3%). August secondary activity +2.7% YoY/+0.2% MoM; tertiary +2.6%/+0.1%. July total +2.8% YoY/+0.6% MoM. Next IOAE: October 20.
- **Model synthesis:** August's 0.1% monthly point estimate is lower than July's 0.6%, but their confidence intervals overlap. The evidence therefore does not establish a slowdown, firm-level demand, or a definitive positive monthly direction.
- **Connection:** La Jornada states that the July monthly estimate changed from 0.1% previously to 0.6% now. No earlier MexicoBrief indicator coverage was identified, so the draft connects the new estimate to the prior reported estimate rather than claiming continuity with a published edition.

## Considered, not included

- **Trade reorientation toward U.S. suppliers:** Reuters reported on September 21 that President Sheinbaum said Mexico plans to buy more U.S. goods and reduce imports from elsewhere during the USMCA review. [Accessible Reuters syndication](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/mexico-plans-to-boost-us-imports-as-part-of-trade-treaty-review-sheinbaum-says-ce785adbdf8afe27)
- **Reason excluded:** the direction was not genuinely new. In an official September 4 transcript, Sheinbaum had already said Mexico was analyzing greater U.S. imports and fewer purchases from other countries. [Presidency transcript](https://www.gob.mx/presidencia/articulos/version-estenografica-conferencia-de-prensa-de-la-presidenta-claudia-sheinbaum-pardo-del-04-de-septiembre-de-2026)

[Presidency release](https://www.gob.mx/presidencia/prensa/avanza-posibilidad-de-acuerdo-mexico-estados-unidos-presidenta-sheinbaum?idiom=es)
- **Decision:** do not convert negotiation posture into procurement implications until measures, product categories, timing, or an agreement are published.
